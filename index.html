About

We build apps that are clear, useful, and easy to use.

Max-Q Studio is an independent mobile app developer focused on practical products for everyday needs. Our apps are designed to help people manage small but important parts of their day without confusing menus or unnecessary features.

We care about clean design, simple language, and features that solve real problems. Each product starts small, gets improved step by step, and grows only when the core experience is ready.
